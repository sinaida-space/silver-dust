Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Silver Dust is a static page. It has no accounts, no analytics and no cookies, and it sends nothing you type to me or to anyone else.

Who is responsible

Sinaida Krivchenko, Prague, Czechia. For any privacy question, write to me through sinaida.eu/privacy.

What data

I do not collect personal data. What you type into the questions is processed in your browser only. The presentation file is built on your device and saved by your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which delivers pages through the Fastly network. When you open a page, the server receives a technical request: your IP address, the time, the address you asked for and your browser type. GitHub processes this to deliver the site and keep it secure. I do not collect or keep these logs.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in running and securing a public website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Transfers outside the EU: GitHub is a US company. It states that it relies on standard contractual clauses and takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

See the GitHub privacy statement.

Third parties

The page does not contact any third-party server. The font and the presentation library are served from this same site.

Local storage

Your answers are kept in your browser’s local storage under the key silver_dust_v2, so a reload does not lose them. They stay on your device, are never transmitted, and remain until you start a new session (the restart button), or clear the site data in your browser. No cookies are set.

Your rights

Your answers exist only in your own browser, so there is no record on my side to request or correct. For connection data held by GitHub you have the rights under GDPR: access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. Write to me through sinaida.eu/privacy, and I pass your request on to GitHub where needed. You may also complain at any time to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, or to the authority in your own country.
